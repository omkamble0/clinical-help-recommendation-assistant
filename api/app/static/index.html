Example:“Can I take Paracetamol with Lisinopril for high blood pressure?”
✓ Generally Safe with MonitoringEveryday plain English

Direct Answer: Yes, Paracetamol is typically the safest everyday pain reliever when taking blood pressure medicine like Lisinopril. Unlike Ibuprofen or Naproxen (NSAIDs), it will not interfere with blood pressure control or stress kidneys.

💬 2 Questions to ask your doctor or pharmacist:
  • “What is my safe maximum daily limit of paracetamol?”
  • “Are any of my other daily pills already combined with acetaminophen?”
